Bio

Changing the digital world, one vertex at a time.

With a degree in Video Games and Multimedia Applications from the Lusófona University of Porto, I am a 3D artist focused on creating visual and interactive experiences. I master the 3D production pipeline, from modeling and retopology to rigging and animation, ensuring assets ready for implementation in game engines or other applications.

Experienced in customer service and product sales, as I currently work part-time at a Worten retail store.

My differentiating factor lies in my multidisciplinary vision: in addition to my technical focus on 3D, I possess solid skills in Sound Design and Game Design principles, which allows me to shape my skills and contribute to smaller teams. This combination allows me not only to create the visual aspect but also to understand how aesthetics, movement, and sound merge to enhance user immersion.

To learn more about my academic and professional background, I am available to talk.
